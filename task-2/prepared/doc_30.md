# R0-4L0

R0-4L0, or simply ROLO, was a white-and-black R-series astromech with a primary black sensor. By 34 ABY during the Cold War, ROLO maintained Resistance X-wings with other upgraded droids, operating from the Resistance base on the planet D'Qar. When Resistance fighters arrived following the Battle of Takodana, ROLO prepared X-wings for their next mission against the First Order.

That mission became ROLO's after it was assigned to Resistance pilot Jessika Pava's X-wing for the strike on Starkiller Base. After the assignment, ROLO performed last-minute checks on Pava's ship, Blue Three of Blue Squadron. Pava and ROLO followed Commander Poe Dameron to the Ilum system.

There, they attacked the superweapon while fighting through waves of TIE fighters. Pava offered to have Blue Three fly cover for Dameron's final attack, but he ordered the pair to draw enemy TIEs away from the trench. After Dameron triggered Starkiller's destruction, Pava and ROLO joined the remaining seven X-wings and returned to celebrate at their base on D'Qar.
