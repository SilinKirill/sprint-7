# R6-D8

R6-D8 was an R6 astromech droid that served as Temmin Wexley's primary astromech. He flew with Wexley in his X-wing during the Battle of Starkiller Base, through the First Order-Resistance War, and into the final battle on the planet Exegol.

## Biography

### Joining the Resistance

After Wexley joined the Resistance in 32 ABY, R6-D8 learned of Mister Bones, Wexley's late reprogrammed B1-series battle droid, after studying his logs. Seeking to please Wexley, R6-D8 tried to make himself more aggressive. However, he did not dare to overwrite the factory-set safety inhibitors in his personality matrix. As a part of the Resistance Starfighter Corps' Black Squadron, R6-D8 participated in the hunt for Lor San Tekka.

### Battle of Starkiller Base

Following the Hosnian Cataclysm in 34 ABY, R6-D8 prepared Wexley's X-wing for battle after commands were issued to ready for an attack on Starkiller Base. Together, R6 and Wexley followed Commander Poe Dameron into hyperspace for the Ilum system. Wexley participated in multiple attack runs on the superweapon while R6-D8 managed repairs from enemy fire. Once Dameron destabilized the main power source, and all remaining Resistance fighters returned to D'Qar.

### Battle of Exegol

By 35 ABY, R6-D8 and Wexley were stationed on the base on the jungle moon Ajan Kloss. After the Resistance determined a route to the planet Exegol, where what became of the First Order gathered, R6-D8 readied the X-wing for combat. R6-D8 and Wexley left the moon with the other couple dozen of starfighters as the Exegol air team. The Resistance led an undermanned mission, and the Resistance pilots were quickly overwhelmed. R6-D8 perished with Wexley when their T-70 X-wing starfighter suffered damage and crashed into a Star Destroyer by the Sith Eternal forces during the Battle of Exegol, moments before the arrival of the Citizens' Fleet.

## Characteristics

R6-D8 was a well seasoned R6-series astromech droid with many missions on its track record. It had a 0.93 meter tall, white body with yellow detailing and two primary sensors, a black photoreceptor and a red sensor built into its angled head. The droid was loyal to Wexley and exhibited aggression at the highest level allowed in Industrial Automaton's programming.
