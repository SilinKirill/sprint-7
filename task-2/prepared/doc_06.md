# R3-T2

R3-T2, or just R3, was an R3-series astromech droid with red, blue, and white paneling. They lived in Mos Eisley spaceport on the planet Tatooine and in 9 BBY stood in one of the streets when a transport carrying three Inquisitors landed nearby. In 1 BBY, the droid passed by a X-34 landspeeder as they moved along Corporations Road in the spaceport.

## Biography

R3-T2, also known as just R3, was an unaffiliated R3-series astromech droid resident of the spaceport of Mos Eisley on the planet Tatooine during the Imperial Era. Over the years, the droid had several owners who kept R3-T2 in order using parts scavenged from members of the Jawa species.

In 9 BBY, the droid was stood among the crowds on one of Mos Eisley's streets when the transport Scythe flew overhead and landed nearby to allow three Sith Inquisitors to disembark, although the droid did not turn to face the Inquisitors with the rest of the crowd.

In 1 BBY, R3-T2 was rolling down Corporations Road in Mos Eisley when an X-34 landspeeder carrying the locals Luke Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi and Skywalker's droids, R2-D2 and C-3PO, passed them. A recklessly piloted Flare-S swoop then caused a ronto to rear up in front of R3-T2, causing a Jawa to fall from the ronto's back in front of the droid. The astromech droid later passed by the same landspeeder again outside Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina.

## Characteristics

Standing 1.09 meters tall, R3-T2 had a black sensor on their red dome and white-and-blue paneling on their chassis. They were part of the R-series manufactured by Industrial Automaton.
