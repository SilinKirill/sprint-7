# U-3PO

U-3PO, also known as Yoo-Threepio, was a 3PO-series protocol droid created by Cybot Galactica prior to the Battle of Yavin. U-3PO served in the Diplomatic Corps of the Royal House of Alderaan during the Galactic Civil War, but eventually became a unwitting sleeper agent for the Galactic Empire.

## Biography

During the time of the Galactic Civil War, U-3PO was reprogrammed to serve the Galactic Empire, making the droid a unwitting sleeper agent. U-3PO was inserted into the Tantive IV's droid pool during the subjugation of Ralltiir. U-3PO later transmitted an S-thread signal to the Empire, allowing the Star Destroyer Devastator to track down the Tantive IV, thus allowing it to be attacked by the Devastator and boarded by Darth Vader and the 501st Legion. As Vader's forces boarded the Tantive IV, U-3PO walked behind C-3PO and R2-D2 as the two droids discussed the imminent attack on the vessel. Shortly after, U-3PO turned into an adjacent doorway.

## Characteristics

U-3PO had silver plating and yellow sensors.
