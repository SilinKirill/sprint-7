# R4-F5

R4-F5 was a blue-green and gray R4-P astromech droid that was permanently integrated into the Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor of Jedi Master Plo Koon. At the end of the Clone Wars, the droid was killed along with its master on the planet Cato Neimoidia when their ship was destroyed by Clone Captain Jag during the execution of Order 66.

## Description

The R-series astromech droid R4-F5 was integrated into Plo Koon's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor starfighter as his navigator and copilot, and therefore only possessed a dome head instead of a full body. This dome was blue-green in color, with gray panels.

## History

In the early stages of the Clone Wars, Jedi Master General Plo Koon, along with the astromech droid R7-D4, flew the Blade of Dorin, a Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor. At some point, Koon began flying a Delta-7 instead, coupled with R4-F5.

Koon flew his Delta-7 alongside R4-F5 during the Battle of Cato Neimoidia shortly before the end of the Clone Wars. The droid was destroyed along with the starfighter by Koon's clone wingmen upon the activation of Order 66 by the Dark Lord of the Sith Darth Sidious, killing Plo Koon as well.
