# R2-Q9

> "This is workplace harassment, I’ll have you know." ―TK-423, to R2-Q9, after the latter threatened them with an arc welder

R2-Q9 was an astromech droid who served onboard the Death Star II battle station in 4 ABY. The droid regularly made trouble for the stormtrooper TK-423, locking them out of their quarters, running over their feet, and even threatening them with an arc welder.

## Biography

R2-Q9 was an R-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that served the Galactic Empire. In 4 ABY, the droid was stationed aboard the Death Star II battle station and was assigned to a specific floor. The floor also had a specific detachment of stormtroopers serving there. When the stormtrooper TK-423 asked if R2-Q9 would serve as the holoprojector to allow their captain to show the holovid Officer & Commander to the unit, R2-Q9 rudely responded in Binary, cursing at them and telling TK-423 that they were a pitiful individual on the lowest level of the Imperial food chain. The droid then proceeded to routinely pick on TK-423, locking the trooper out of their quarters and also locking them inside the refresher. R2-Q9 would also regularly travel through corridors at high speeds when passing TK-423's unit, which caused several accidents.

When TK-423 attempted to file a report against R2-Q9's disruptive behavior, the form disappeared, causing TK-423 to suspect the astromech droid had destroyed it. Later, TK-423's companion TK-293 agreed to switch uniforms with them in hopes of thwarting R2-Q9's antics. However, the droid was still able to single out TK-423 and run over their foot. R2-Q9 was even emboldened enough to threaten TK-423 with its arc welder, forcing the trooper to hand over the food they had gathered from the cafeteria before throwing it out the trash compactor. Tk-423 would detail each of the incidents with the astromech in their log.

The day of the Rebel Alliance attack on the Death Star II, R2-Q9 chased down TK-423 while the trooper was collecting equipment. As TK-423 fled, R2-Q9 attempted to shut the doors they were fleeing through. When TK-423 eventually hid inside an escape pod, R2-Q9 pushed the launch button, looking on as the helpless trooper was sent flying into the chaos of the space battle.
