# R5-J2

> "Lord Vader, this is an unexpected pleasure. We're honored by your presence." ―Tiaan Jerjerrod greets Vader on behalf of R5-J2 and the others present

R5-J2 was an Imperial modified R5 series astromech droid stationed on board the Death Star II superweapon while the battle station was under construction in the Endor system. The droid was present in one of the Death Star's hangars for the arrival of the Sith Lord Darth Vader on board the station in 4 ABY. R5-J2 was then also present in the hangar for the arrival of Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine shortly before the Alliance to Restore the Republic attacked the Death Star. The astromech was still stationed on board the superweapon during the battle, which ended with the station's destruction.

## Biography

R5-J2 was an R5 series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton that served the Galactic Empire during its war against the Alliance to Restore the Republic. In 4 ABY, the R-series astromech droid was stationed on board the Death Star II superweapon while it was under construction above the moon Endor in the Endor system. The droid stood beside a similar unit near the wall of one of the Death Star's hangars as part of the group of Imperial personnel led by Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod that were present for the arrival of the Sith Lord Darth Vader when he arrived on the battle station to speed up its construction.

R5-J2 also witnessed the momentous arrival of Galactic Emperor Sheev Palpatine in Hangar 272 on board the Death Star sometime after Vader arrived. The droid stood in a similar position near the wall as to when Vader arrived, but this time in a larger group of droids. The astromech was still stationed on the Death Star when the Alliance Fleet attacked the station shortly after the Emperor's arrival and destroyed it, with Palpatine also perishing during the battle.

## Characteristics

R5-J2 was a modified R5 unit and had primarily sleek black paneling with some copper sections. The droid had two black sensors on its dome and stood 0.97 meters tall.
