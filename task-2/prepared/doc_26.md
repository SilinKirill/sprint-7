# WED-15-77

WED-15-77 was a WED-15-XT Treadwell repair droid that served the Lars family at their moisture farm on the planet Tatooine for more than twenty years. Built sometime prior to the Clone Wars, WED-15-77 was responsible for many jobs at the Lars family moisture farm, including helping the Lars' nephew Luke Skywalker to repair and maintain his uncle's moisture vaporators.

## Biography

WED-15-77 was a WED-15-XT Treadwell repair droid. Sometime prior to the Clone Wars, which broke out in 22 BBY, the droid began to serve moisture farmer Owen Lars' family at their moisture farm on the planet Tatooine. The droid worked with the protocol droid C-3PO for several years before the Jedi Anakin Skywalker, who had built C-3PO as a child, visited the Lars family at their moisture farm and took him away.

The droid continued to serve the Lars family for more than twenty years, leading into the time of the Galactic Civil War. It would do jobs such as helping the Lars' nephew Luke Skywalker with a variety of chores, including repair work on his uncle's moisture vaporators out in his desert. Although it appreciated working with Owen and Luke, WED-15-77 preferred working for Beru, as she would always ask it to do the same predictable jobs.

One day in 1 BBY, WED-15-77 accompanied Luke Skywalker to repair his uncle's vaporators in the desert when Luke caught sight of a battle in the skies above between the Tantive IV and the Devastator. After that, this droid broken down, but its master abandoned it in the desert. And WED-15-77 was subsequently taken by the scavengers of the desert.
