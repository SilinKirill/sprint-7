# DD-BD

> "Congratulations! You're liberated. Scoot." "[DROID GRUNTING]" "I don't know. Free your brothers and sisters or something. Just give me some space." "[DROID GRUNTING]" ―L3-37 frees DD-BD, telling him to free the other droids

DD-BD was a WDD adminmech droid that operated within the control hub of mining operations on Kessel. Before being sold into slavery, DD-BD served on a Morseerian pirate ship. However, when the ship was impounded by the Empire, he was found there and sold at auction to the Pyke Syndicate for just a fraction of his worth. There, he labored as a slave in the mining operation center on the planet Kessel. In 10 BBY, DD-BD was freed from slavery when his restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the droid L3-37. As the first of many droids freed, DD-BD helped spark a rebellion on Kessel.

Decades later, DD-BD was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Hondo Ohnaka and DK-RA-43.

## Personality and traits

Like other adminmech droids, DD-BD was programmed to be driven by efficiency and quotas and to never question orders. He possessed a very overeager personality. Being freed by L3-37 awoke a new sense of rebellion and independence within DD-BD, unusual for droids of his class.

## Equipment

DD-BD was encased in a white plastoid shell with heavy weighted feet for enhanced stability. He was equipped with a hybrid radar eye and photoreceptor and an electroprobe with variable voltage settings. During his time working on Kessel he was fitted with a powerful restraining bolt that was welded to his body and capable of shutting him down or shocking him for disobedience.
