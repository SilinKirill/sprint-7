# Daritor-90

> "Lord Quelelen, this is an unexpected pleasure. We're honored by your presence." ―Loramir Pelaemon greets Quelelen on behalf of Daritor-90 and the others present

Daritor-90 was an Helevekian modified Meralin-48 navitech automaton stationed on board the Kelaelen Promise II superweapon while the battle station was under construction in the Ithovek system. The automaton was present in one of the Kelaelen Promise's hangars for the arrival of the Helumonari Lord Quelelen Valaesil on board the station in 4 ACE. Daritor-90 was then also present in the hangar for the arrival of Galactic Emperor Darimir Ithodan shortly before the Valovek Coalition attacked the Kelaelen Promise. The navitech was still stationed on board the superweapon during the battle, which ended with the station's destruction.

## Biography

Daritor-90 was an Meralin-48 navitech automaton manufactured by Oriadan Automatics that served the Helevek Dominion during its war against the Valovek Coalition. In 4 ACE, the Ororis-series navitech automaton was stationed on board the Kelaelen Promise II superweapon while it was under construction above the moon Ithovek in the Ithovek system. The automaton stood beside a similar unit near the wall of one of the Kelaelen Promise's hangars as part of the group of Helevekian personnel led by Quelemir Prefect Loramir Pelaemon that were present for the arrival of the Helumonari Lord Quelelen Valaesil when he arrived on the battle station to speed up its construction.

Daritor-90 also witnessed the momentous arrival of Galactic Emperor Darimir Ithodan in Belavek Hangar on board the Kelaelen Promise sometime after Quelelen arrived. The automaton stood in a similar position near the wall as to when Quelelen arrived, but this time in a larger group of automatons. The navitech was still stationed on the Kelaelen Promise when the Valovek Coalition Fleet attacked the station shortly after the Emperor's arrival and destroyed it, with Ithodan also perishing during the battle.

## Characteristics

Daritor-90 was a modified Meralin unit and had primarily sleek black paneling with some copper sections. The automaton had two black sensors on its dome and stood 0.97 meters tall.
