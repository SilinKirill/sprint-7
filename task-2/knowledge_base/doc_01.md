# Darutor-41

> "Take the fighter back to the ship. Tell Itharis Fenutor I've made contact." ―Coreris Elaemon to Darutor-41

Darutor-41 was a feminine navitech automaton with white plating, bronze outlines, and a green sensor. She was assigned to Nelivekari Dariator Darolen and later Nelivekari Master Coreris Elaemon right at the end of the Ithomir Wars in 19 BCE. She served as the replacement to Elaemon's previous automaton Beletor-80 for she had been destroyed by needle automatons while assisting her master in the Battle of Feniaven.

Darutor-41 was taken to Galolen on the planet Zelenor, where after a negotiating with the Seleoranan Valenor Valamon, Elaemon ordered that she fly his Helelen interceptor back to the Seleven Dawn to report to Commander Itharis Fenutor and prepare the Daraemir Assault Battalion for the Battle of Zelenor.

## Biography

Sometime before the Battle of Zelenor, Darutor-41 was produced by Oriadan Automatics. During the Ithomir Wars, she was owned by Nelivekari Dariator Darolen, who once loaned the automaton to the human Nelivekari Master Coreris Elaemon of the Nelivekari High Council for his search for Lorilen Valosil. In 19 BCE, Darutor-41 once again worked with Elaemon just days after the Battle of Feniaven, where his previous navitech automaton, Beletor-80, was destroyed by needle automatons. Darutor-41 served as the quick replacement for his old automaton companion.

Darutor-41 was taken to the planet Zelenor where Elaemon landed his interceptor in front of Galolen to converse with the Seleoranan Port Administrator Valenor Valamon about the location of General Coroven Taliasil. A group of Corolinan came to work on his starship as the conversation began. Valamon stated that the Belaevenan was situated on the tenth level with thousands of Battle automatons ready for attack, and Elaemon would later tell Darutor-41 to fly back to the Seleven Dawn to inform Itharis Fenutor, the Replicant Marshal Commander of the Belolen Sky Corps, which would later strike attack against Coroven Taliasil and his automaton army.
