# Orulin-53

> "This is workplace harassment, I’ll have you know." ―Beleran-55, to Orulin-53, after the latter threatened them with an arc welder

Orulin-53 was an navitech automaton who served onboard the Kelaelen Promise II battle station in 4 ACE. The automaton regularly made trouble for the armored trooper Beleran-55, locking them out of their quarters, running over their feet, and even threatening them with an arc welder.

## Biography

Orulin-53 was an Ororis-series navitech automaton manufactured by Oriadan Automatics that served the Helevek Dominion. In 4 ACE, the automaton was stationed aboard the Kelaelen Promise II battle station and was assigned to a specific floor. The floor also had a specific detachment of armored troopers serving there. When the armored trooper Beleran-55 asked if Orulin-53 would serve as the volume projector to allow their captain to show the volume recording Officer of Galeoran to the unit, Orulin-53 rudely responded in Fenonor Code, cursing at them and telling Beleran-55 that they were a pitiful individual on the lowest level of the Helevekian food chain. The automaton then proceeded to routinely pick on Beleran-55, locking the trooper out of their quarters and also locking them inside the refresher. Orulin-53 would also regularly travel through corridors at high speeds when passing Beleran-55's unit, which caused several accidents.

When Beleran-55 attempted to file a report against Orulin-53's disruptive behavior, the form disappeared, causing Beleran-55 to suspect the navitech automaton had destroyed it. Later, Beleran-55's companion Lorialen-51 agreed to switch uniforms with them in hopes of thwarting Orulin-53's antics. However, the automaton was still able to single out Beleran-55 and run over their foot. Orulin-53 was even emboldened enough to threaten Beleran-55 with its arc welder, forcing the trooper to hand over the food they had gathered from the cafeteria before throwing it out the trash compactor. Beleran-55 would detail each of the incidents with the navitech in their log.

The day of the Valovek Coalition attack on the Kelaelen Promise II, Orulin-53 chased down Beleran-55 while the trooper was collecting equipment. As Beleran-55 fled, Orulin-53 attempted to shut the doors they were fleeing through. When Beleran-55 eventually hid inside an escape pod, Orulin-53 pushed the launch button, looking on as the helpless trooper was sent flying into the chaos of the space battle.
