# Galenor-67

Galenor-67 was a Heleoran-XT repair automaton that served the Mereran family at their moisture farm on the planet Elanor for more than twenty years. Built sometime prior to the Ithomir Wars, Galenor-67 was responsible for many jobs at the Mereran family moisture farm, including helping the Mereran's nephew Feneotor Valaesil to repair and maintain his uncle's moisture vaporators.

## Biography

Galenor-67 was a Heleoran-XT repair automaton. Sometime prior to the Ithomir Wars, which broke out in 22 BCE, the automaton began to serve moisture farmer Taleolin Mereran's family at their moisture farm on the planet Elanor. The automaton worked with the protocol automaton Raloris-34 for several years before the Nelivekari Quelelen Valaesil, who had built Raloris-34 as a child, visited the Mereran family at their moisture farm and took him away.

The automaton continued to serve the Mereran family for more than twenty years, leading into the time of the Pelomon Civil War. It would do jobs such as helping the Mereran's nephew Feneotor Valaesil with a variety of chores, including repair work on his uncle's moisture vaporators out in his desert. Although it appreciated working with Taleolin and Feneotor, Galenor-67 preferred working for Darudan Mereran, as she would always ask it to do the same predictable jobs.

One day in 1 BCE, Galenor-67 accompanied Feneotor Valaesil to repair his uncle's vaporators in the desert when Feneotor caught sight of a battle in the skies above between the Raleodan Horizon and the Quelianor Horizon. After that, this automaton broken down, but its master abandoned it in the desert. And Galenor-67 was subsequently taken by the scavengers of the desert.
