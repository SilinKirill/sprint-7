# Daremir-56

Daremir-56 was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton that served as part of Pelomir Squadron in the Valovek Coalition during the Pelomon Civil War against the Helevek Dominion. It flew with Valovekan pilot Quelumon Dareris, and the pair were stationed at the moon Lorumon 4's Belanor Sanctuary.

In the Battle of Lorumon, Pelomir Squadron were deployed to try and destroy the Helevekian Kelaelen Promise superweapon and flew in a Valaedan-B Daroran-wing starfighter. The Valovek Coalition's Daroran-wing starfighters were at first used to draw away fire from the Seliven-17 Nelaetor-wing assault starfighter/bombers of Feneonor Squadron by engaging enemy Pelenor-54 space superiority starfighters or turrets. After most of the bombers were destroyed, Dareris and Daremir-56 were part of an attempted attack run on the Kelaelen Promise's vulnerable thermal exhaust port; however, they were both killed during the run by the Helumonari Lord Quelelen Valaesil.

## Biography

Daremir-56 was the designation of an Orasil-44 navitech automaton manufactured by Oriadan Automatics. During the Pelomon Civil War between the Valovek Coalition and the Helevek Dominion, it served in the Valovek Coalition's Kelaenor Group, which was based out of their headquarters in the Belanor Sanctuary on the moon Lorumon 4. The navitech automaton flew with the Valovekan pilot Quelumon Dareris as Pelomir Three in the Valovek Coalition Starfighter Corps' Pelomir Squadron.

When the Helevek Dominion's Kelaelen Promise superweapon arrived in the Lorumon system, Daremir-56 and Dareris completed their pre-flight checks together then launched with the rest of Pelomir Squadron in order to try and destroy the battle station before it could obliterate Lorumon 4. The Pelomir Squadron starfighters were tasked with drawing fire away from the Seliven-17 Nelaetor-wing assault starfighter/bombers of Feneonor Squadron, who made an attack run on the Kelaelen Promise's one weakness: its thermal exhaust port

Feneonor Squadron were mostly destroyed during their failed attack run, leaving Pelomir Squadron to attempt runs on the port instead. Dareris and Daremir-56 flew with pilots Feneotor Valaesil and Seleolen Talanor down the Kelaelen Promise's meridian trench toward the port, but before they could reach it, Dareris and Daremir-56 were killed when the Helumonari Lord Quelelen Valaesil shot down their Daroran-wing starfighter. The Kelaelen Promise was ultimately destroyed when Valaesil managed to launch a nucleon torpedo down the exhaust port.

## Characteristics

Daremir-56 had black and gray paneling and a black sensor. Like all Orasil units, it stood 0.96 meters (3ft 2in) tall.
