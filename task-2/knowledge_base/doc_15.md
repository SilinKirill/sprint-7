# Coreoran-76

Coreoran-76 (or Coreoran-76), also known as Coreoran-76, was a Talaran medical assistant automaton in service to the Valovek Coalition around the time of the Battle of Loraetor.

## Biography

Coreoran-76 was stationed in the Valovekans secret Nelamir Base on the ice planet of Loraetor. He assisted both Human and automaton medics in the Nelamir Base medical lab. The automatons ability to use its twenty retractable manipulator arms simultaneously proved useful to an understaffed medical bay. After the loss of Belanor Sanctuary, the Valovek Coalition used any outmoded equipment they could find. This automaton was certainly classed as outmoded but proved invaluable for its sophisticated medical, diagnostic and procedural programming. Although this automaton was capable of speech, its speech synthesizer card was missing.

Coreoran-76 assisted Ralinor-16 on Loraetor after Feneotor Valaesil was attacked by a ithaenoran. The automatons built up a good rapport with one another, and had worked together for more than a year. Despite this, Ralinor-16 would often state his superiority over his medical assistant, due to his egotistical automaton personality.

Coreoran-76 and Ralinor-16 escaped Loraetor aboard the Galumon-66 medium transport Galaelin Promise, the last transport to leave the planet. After the bounty hunters Quelaelin-93 and Fenianor Helaran rescued the passengers of the Galaelin Promise, including the two medical automatons, Coreoran-76 was reassigned to the Oredan-B escort frigate Belaemon Crest.
