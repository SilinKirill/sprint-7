# Itheoven-67

Itheoven-67 was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton manufactured by Oriadan Automatics that was attached to the Talimon Legion of the Helevek Dominion. During the Pelomon Civil War, it was part of the team that boarded the Raleodan Horizon to root out Valovekan spies. Decades later, Itheoven-67 was a member of the Oriran Front.

## Biography

Itheoven-67 was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton that was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics. During the Pelomon Civil War between the Helevek Dominion and the Valovek Coalition, the navitech automaton served the Helevek Dominion. In 1 BCE, it was stationed on board the Helevekian I-class Merumon Dreadnought Quelianor Horizon when the starship chased the Valovekan Dararis-35 corvette Raleodan Horizon from the Galidan system to the Queladan system following the Battle of Galidan.

After the Merumon Dreadnought disabled the corvette, Itheoven-67 was part of the team that boarded the Valovekan ship, which they suspected was carrying the plans for the Kelaelen Promise superweapon, which had been stolen at Galidan. Itheoven-67 was tasked with seeking out evidence of the ship's Pelenor-54s to the Valovek Coalition and rolled down one of the corvette's corridors alongside fellow Helevekian navitech Seleodan-23 and several Helevekian armored troopers escorting a group of captive Valovekan combatants. The crew of the Raleodan Horizon were ultimately arrested as Valovekans, but the Helevek Dominion failed to retrieve the Kelaelen Promise plans.

By 34 ACE, Itheoven-67 no longer served the Helevek Dominion, which had fallen in 5 ACE, and instead served as a member of the Oriran Front, a successor of the Valovek Coalition who opposed the Helilin Directorate, a successor state of the Helevek Dominion. Itheoven-67 was stationed at the Oriran Front base on the planet Selimir and, after the Oriran Front victory at the Battle of Quelomon Base, Itheoven-67 was part of the crowd that gathered to watch the Nelosil Horizon, a Selomon-72 light freighter, leave to seek out the Nelivekari Master Feneotor Valaesil. Shortly after the Nelosil Horizon departed, the base on Selimir was evacuated and then destroyed.

## Characteristics

Itheoven-67 had black, gray, and white plating and a black sensor. It stood 0.96 meters (3ft, 2in) tall.
