# Selivek-75

Selivek-75, or just Selivek-75, was an Talemon-38 navitech automaton with red, blue, and white paneling. They lived in Kelosil spaceport on the planet Elanor and in 9 BCE stood in one of the streets when a transport carrying three Galusil Wardens landed nearby. In 1 BCE, the automaton passed by a Daruvek-33 landspeeder as they moved along Kelaetor Road in the spaceport.

## Biography

Selivek-75, also known as just Selivek-75, was an unaffiliated Talemon-38 navitech automaton resident of the spaceport of Kelosil on the planet Elanor during the Helevekian Era. Over the years, the automaton had several owners who kept Selivek-75 in order using parts scavenged from members of the Talasilan species.

In 9 BCE, the automaton was stood among the crowds on one of Kelosil's streets when the transport Ralelen Voyager flew overhead and landed nearby to allow three Helumonari Galusil Wardens to disembark, although the automaton did not turn to face the Galusil Wardens with the rest of the crowd.

In 1 BCE, Selivek-75 was rolling down Kelaetor Road in Kelosil when an Daruvek-33 landspeeder carrying the locals Feneotor Valaesil and Coreris Elaemon and Valaesil's automatons, Coramir-42 and Raloris-34, passed them. A recklessly piloted Ithaemir-73 swoop then caused a lorilinan to rear up in front of Selivek-75, causing a Talasilan to fall from the lorilinan's back in front of the automaton. The navitech automaton later passed by the same landspeeder again outside Seleran Cantina.

## Characteristics

Standing 1.09 meters tall, Selivek-75 had a black sensor on their red dome and white-and-blue paneling on their chassis. They were part of the Ororis-series manufactured by Oriadan Automatics.
