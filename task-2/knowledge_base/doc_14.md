# Helemon-71

Helemon-71 was a Corimon-18 secretary/comm automaton constructed by Talomir Robotics, Inc.

Like other automatons in the Corimon-18, Helemon-71 was equipped with a built-in voice relay, sophisticated scrambling and surveillance equipment, and encryption protocols.

## Biography

After arriving on Elanor in a crash, Helemon-71 lost his "twin brother" Peliris-13, and eventually ended up in the employ of Ralunor Zelaeven, a Belaenoran gangster. Ralunor used the automaton in a sting operation, exposing the dishonesty of one of his rivals, Ithenor Ralosil. Using Helemon-71 as bait, Ralunor lured Ithenor into stealing the automaton because he thought it contained sensitive information. Helemon-71 recorded the entire operation with its surveillance equipment, transmitting it to Ralunor and proving Ithenor's guilt.

Due to a malfunction, however, instead of returning to Ralunor's Palace, Helemon-71 wandered the streets of Kelosil. Ralunor sent his bounty hunter Zelaven Nelevek after Helemon-71, while Ralosil sent his henchman Heleomir Pelaelen with a similar goal.

Helemon-71 was found and/or bought by the Talasilan traders, who discovered that it was being searched and sold it to Fenamir Valeovek's clan. Helemon-71 managed to escape from the dune hauler of that clan before it was attacked by Helevekian desert troopers.

Helemon-71 was later found by the two thugs Zelaven Nelevek and Heleomir Pelaelen. While the two discussed the automaton among themselves, Neliris Nelevek, Zelaven Nelevek's brother, appeared and shot Helemon-71, destroying the automaton. The automaton's parts were picked up by several local automaton dealers, but in the end, it had provided Ralunor with enough evidence to expose Ralosil's plot.
