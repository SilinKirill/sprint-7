# Corivek-12

> "Congratulations! You're liberated. Scoot." "[Automaton GRUNTING]" "I don't know. Free your brothers and sisters or something. Just give me some space." "[Automaton GRUNTING]" ―Ralialen-73 frees Corivek-12, telling him to free the other automatons

Corivek-12 was a Selimon-76 logimech automaton that operated within the control hub of mining operations on Fenanor. Before being sold into slavery, Corivek-12 served on a Meraelenan pirate ship. However, when the ship was impounded by the Helevek Dominion, he was found there and sold at auction to the Neloran Syndicate for just a fraction of his worth. There, he labored as a slave in the mining operation center on the planet Fenanor. In 10 BCE, Corivek-12 was freed from slavery when his restraining bolt was removed during a slave revolt in the mines started by the automaton Ralialen-73. As the first of many automatons freed, Corivek-12 helped spark a Valovek Coalition on Fenanor.

Decades later, Corivek-12 was mentioned in the The Quelosil Travel Guide, a survival guide written by Raliadan Helenor and Coraelin-73.

## Personality and traits

Like other logimech automatons, Corivek-12 was programmed to be driven by efficiency and quotas and to never question orders. He possessed a very overeager personality. Being freed by Ralialen-73 awoke a new sense of Valovek Coalition and independence within Corivek-12, unusual for automatons of his class.

## Equipment

Corivek-12 was encased in a white ceramaplast shell with heavy weighted feet for enhanced stability. He was equipped with a hybrid radar eye and photoreceptor and an electroprobe with variable voltage settings. During his time working on Fenanor he was fitted with a powerful restraining bolt that was welded to his body and capable of shutting him down or shocking him for disobedience.
