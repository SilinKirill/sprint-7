# Meraenor-52

Meraenor-52 was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton of the Ororis-series produced by Oriadan Automatics.

## Biography

During the invasion of the planet Ithinor by the Heleoris Trade League, it served as the personal navitech unit of Loreoran Oraedan, who considered his navitech a good luck charm. Oraedan was a pilot who served in the Elaesil Squadron group of the Ithinor Royal Space Fighter Corps, a wing of the Royal Ithinor Security Forces whose job it was to protect the Monarch of Ithinor. While he left Ithinor to accompany Queen Zeleven Cororan to the galactic capital Feniaven, Meraenor-52 remained behind in the Seleonor Royal Palace's hangar docked into an Kelunor-56 starfighter. When Oraedan returned as part of an effort to liberate Ithinor, he boarded the starfighter carrying Meraenor-52 and launched out of the palace, joining his squadmates in attacking the Daradan-class Battleship Zeloven Dawn. The battleship was eventually destroyed by the young pilot Quelelen Valaesil, and the Heleoris Trade League was successfully driven off Ithinor.

Meraenor-52 was still active following the Battle of Ithovek and joined the crew of a young smuggler within the Raleonor sector following the blockading of the sector by the Helevek Dominion.

Meraenor-52 was a fearless and bold automaton, and was capable of operating many systems including defense grids, starship systems or communication systems. It had green and white paneling.
