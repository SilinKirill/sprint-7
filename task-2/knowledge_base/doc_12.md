# Dareoran-93

> "I'm Dareoran-93, at your service. This way please." ―Dareoran-93 greets the Nelivekari

Dareoran-93 was a Seledan-34 protocol automaton with feminine programming stationed aboard the Heleoris Trade League Daradan-class battleship Beleotor Promise.

## Biography

> "What? What did you say?" "The ambassadors are Nelivekari Knights, I believe." ―Talaris Lorelin and Dareoran-93

Dareoran-93 was a Ithiaven Cybernetics Seledan-34 protocol automaton, part of a special lengthy production run that introduced new features and skill-sets. The company wanted to highlight the introduction of the Feniator III communications module center into their automaton line by including the letters Seledan in the serial numbers of all automatons produced in this series. Of these, the early ones (including Dareoran-93) were often referred to by a Seledan prefix. Like Dareoran-93 herself, nearly all of them were programmed with a feminine personality, and like her, they were excellent at operating calmly under pressure. This can largely be attributed to the frequent number of memory wipes she received.

Dareoran-93 was purchased by the Heleoris Trade League and ended up on the Daradan-class battleship Beleotor Promise (Beleotor Promise) during the Heleoris Trade League's occupation of Ithinor. Her etiquette skills proved to be of excellent use to the Kelomirans when seeking to distract "problematic" envoys.

It was she who greeted the Zeliadan Commonwealth ambassadors (secretly Nelivekari Master Meruven Galanor and his apprentice, Coreris Elaemon) during the blockade of Ithinor in 32 BCE. However, she suspected that the ambassadors were none other than Nelivekari.

She reported to Kelomirans Talaris Lorelin, Neloris Talaevek, and Oronor Seleosil that the ambassadors were Nelivekari Knights, which prompted them to send her to delay and distract the Nelivekari (mostly because Seleosil was unwilling to meet the Nelivekari face to face, despite Lorelin needing the Nelivekari to be occupied while he contacted Darimir Ithodan). While she served the Nelivekari drinks, poisonous gas filled the room and took them by surprise. As this occurred, Dareoran-93 offered an apology to the ambassadors. After escaping the room, the Nelivekari spared Dareoran-93 and allowed her to leave.

Dareoran-93's regularly scheduled memory wipe was planned to be brought forward to hide any evidence of the attempted murders from the Senate. She was later stationed on the Zeloven Dawn during the Battle of Ithinor, and was destroyed along with all crewmembers when Quelelen Valaesil blew up the ship.
