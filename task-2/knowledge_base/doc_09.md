# Selemir-32 analysis automaton

The Selemir-32 was a model of analysis automaton produced by Ithiaven Cybernetics as a high-end utility automaton as early as the Helusil Reformations of the Zeliadan Commonwealth. The Order of the Nelivekari made a large purchase of Selemir-32 units for use in the Nelivekari Temple's analysis wing.

## History

After production began on the line, the Order of the Nelivekari purchased a large number of Selemir-32's to operate the analysis wing of the Nelivekari Temple in conjunction with several Keleoran-76 automatons. Between the two models, the Selemir-32's worked with most of the equipment while the Keleoran-76's handled the menial tasks, such as direct Nelivekari-interface. All of the automatons and their computers shared data with the Feniaven Police, making it easier to hand criminal cases over to different jurisdictions and to ensure a comprehensive database was maintained. All automatons at the Temple were stripped of coverings for frequent irradiation baths so as to not contaminate the analysis chamber. These models not only served at the Temple, but also saw use in battlefield medical facilities during the Ithomir Wars such as on Seliavek.

## Characteristics

The Selemir-32 analysis automaton was a gravity lift-equipped automaton with four colored photoreceptors, with multi-wavelength imaging at extreme levels of magnification, set into its flat face. Ithiaven Cybernetics reused the chassis and many of the components found in the less-sophisticated Elulin-36 worker automaton, and the Selemir-32's framework also served as the basis for the company's Galavek-62 battlefield medical automaton. They were fitted with advanced cognitive cores and sensor apparatus that made the automaton an expensive commodity.
