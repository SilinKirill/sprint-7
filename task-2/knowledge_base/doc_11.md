# Taloven-77

Taloven-77 was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton produced by Oriadan Automatics and working for the Helevek Dominion. Taloven-77 had black coverings highlighted by bronze access panels, and was a well-equipped automaton with tools for repairing starships and special, hidden spy devices. The automaton also possessed a hidden pulse emitter cannon under one of its dome flaps.

Taloven-77 was charged with storing the Emperor's plans against the Valovek Coalition within its memory bank as well as being personally responsible for programming slipspace co-ordinates directly into Emperor Ithodan's shuttle during covert missions.

## Biography

In the year 2 BCE, Taloven-77 was present in the Neleomir Lounge bar on Pelaemir, where the automaton was caught in a firefight between Helevekian troops and Meralen Beliris, who had traveled to Pelaemir to find Nelivekari General Zeliamir Kelaemon, on the order of Senator Eleoven Raleoran.

Taloven-77 also became aware of Helunor-89's infiltration of the Kelaelen Promise II and subsequent plan to reprogram every Helevekian automaton to follow his commands. The automaton took it upon itself to prevent Helunor-89's plans. It was also present during Quelelen Valaesil's unexpected arrival at the station to pressure Quelemir Prefect Loramir Pelaemon with finishing the weapons systems of the station prior to the Emperor's inspection of the station, and was also present among the other automatons when Emperor Ithodan's shuttle arrived at the Kelaelen Promise's Belavek Hangar shortly afterward.

A subsequent Valovekan attack destroyed corridors in the second Kelaelen Promise, resulting in the immobilization of an Eleoran-60 agromech automaton that was carrying a vital message for the Emperor. Taloven-77 was forced to download the data from the Eleoran and deliver it personally. Whether the automaton fulfilled the mission, prior to the Emperor's death, is unknown.
