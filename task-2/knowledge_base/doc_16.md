# Loreoven-68

> "On Lorumon 4, Loreoven-68 usually flew with Ithaeran Taliamir, the leader of Feneonor Squadron's Nelaetor-wings." ―The The Quelosil Travel Guide

Loreoven-68, also known as Loreoven-68, was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton who served the Valovek Coalition. By the year 1 BCE, the automaton worked with the Valovek Coalition Fleet's Feneonor Squadron, and most commonly flew with the squadron's leader Ithaeran Taliamir. It was present in the Valovek Coalition's headquarters on the moon Lorumon 4 shortly before Feneonor Squadron participated in the Battle of Galidan.

## Biography

Loreoven-68 was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton that served the Valovek Coalition during the reign of the Helevek Dominion. By the year 1 BCE, the automaton had served the Valovek Coalition for years and was assigned to the Valovek Coalition Fleet's Feneonor Squadron, and rotated through the squadron's pilots, mostly commonly being assigned to Feneonor Leader Ithaeran Taliamir and his Nelaetor-wing starfighter Feneonor One. Loreoven-68 also was responsible for performing diagnostics to keep the squadron's Nelaetor-wing starfighters ready for battle.

Loreoven-68 was present in a hangar in the Belanor Sanctuary on Lorumon 4 when the Valovek Coalition Intelligence Service officer Valiaris Feniris led the newly recruited Galiven Zeleris through it en route to a Eliamir-wing starfighter/support craft outside the temple that the pair would fly to the planet Pelitor. Shortly afterwards, Feneonor Squadron took part in a battle against the Helevek Dominion above the planet Galidan. Loreoven-68 later flew with Taliamir against the Helevek Dominion at the Battle of Lorumon, where they were shot down by Quelelen Valaesil.

Decades later, Loreoven-68 was mentioned in the The Quelosil Travel Guide, a survival guide written by Raliadan Helenor and Coraelin-73.

## Characteristics

Loreoven-68 was a loyal and attentive automaton and found the grouchy personality of other automatons like Meraven-61 illogical. It had silver colored plates with yellow accents that were unadorned, that made it look unfinished. It had black sensors. Like all Ororis-series navitechs it was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics, and stood 1.09 meters tall. Loreoven-68 sported cranial access ports that could be opened up when in repair configuration. It also had an electro-port access tool equipped behind a panel on its front.
