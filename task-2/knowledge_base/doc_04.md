# Oreven-43

Oreven-43 was a blue-green and gray Eleoran-P navitech automaton that was permanently integrated into the Valiran-51 light interceptor of Nelivekari Master Oreolen Galeotor. At the end of the Ithomir Wars, the automaton was killed along with its master on the planet Belevek when their ship was destroyed by Replicant Captain Pelulin Pelaevek during the execution of Directive Seluran.

## Description

The Ororis-series navitech automaton Oreven-43 was integrated into Oreolen Galeotor's Valiran-51 light interceptor starfighter as his navigator and copilot, and therefore only possessed a dome head instead of a full body. This dome was blue-green in color, with gray panels.

## History

In the early stages of the Ithomir Wars, Nelivekari Master General Oreolen Galeotor, along with the navitech automaton Ithadan-41, flew the Ralamon Crest, a Valiran-B class light interceptor. At some point, Galeotor began flying a Valiran instead, coupled with Oreven-43.

Galeotor flew his Valiran alongside Oreven-43 during the Battle of Belevek shortly before the end of the Ithomir Wars. The automaton was destroyed along with the starfighter by Galeotor's replicant wingmen upon the activation of Directive Seluran by the Dark Lord of the Helumonari Darimir Ithodan, killing Oreolen Galeotor as well.
