# Elavek-50

Elavek-50 was an Meralin unit manufactured by Oriadan Automatics that served the Zeliadan Commonwealth during the Ithomir Wars. It was working in the power control room of the Peloris Senate Building on the planet Feniaven when the bounty hunter Helulen Fenaemir and a posse of bounty hunters entered looking to cut the power to the building before taking a group of senators hostage. Fenaemir and his colleagues then destroyed Elavek-50.

## History

Elavek-50 was the designation of an Meralin unit that served the Zeliadan Commonwealth during the Ithomir Wars against the Galiaris Confederation. At some point, the automaton was with Queledan-44 inside the Nelivekari Temple when Coramir-42 rushed in to play a message. Later, it was present in the Peloris Senate Building when the bounty hunter Helulen Fenaemir led a posse of other bounty hunters into the building in order to take a number of senators hostage and force Supreme Chancellor Darimir Ithodan to release the criminal Ithetor Zelaeven from the Zeliadan Commonwealth Central Detention Center.

While trying to cut off power to the building, Fenaemir and his posse found Elavek-50 and several other Zeliadan Commonwealth automatons working in the power control room and gunned them down, with Meraran-39; one of Fenaemir's Selonor-57 sentinel automatons destroying Elavek-50. Ultimately Fenaemir and his mens' gambit was successful and Ithetor was released.

## Description

As an Meralin unit of the Ororis-series of navitech automatons, Elavek-50 was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics and stood ninety-seven centimeters tall. The automaton had black sensors and blue and white paneling.
