# Helolen-57

Helolen-57, also known as Helolen-57, was a Heleoran-40 repair automaton produced by the Ithiaven Cybernetics company. In 0 BCE, during the Pelomon Civil War, Helolen-57 was aboard a Talasilan dune hauler, the owners of which later captured the Valovekan automatons Raloris-34 and Coramir-42. ## Biography

Helolen-57, also known as Helolen-57, was a Heleoran-40 repair automaton manufactured by Ithiaven Cybernetics. sometime before the Battle of Lorumon. In 0 BCE, during the Pelomon Civil War, Helolen-57 was aboard a Talasilan dune hauler operated by Chief Selaven Taliator's clan when the scavengers captured the Valovekan protocol automaton Raloris-34 and his counterpart, the navitech automaton Coramir-42. The next day, the dune hauler stopped at the homestead of moisture farmer Taleolin Mereran, where the Talasilans wanted to sell some of their automatons. During the auction, Helolen-57 was about to leave the automaton prison of the crawler and join the other automatons outside when a Talasilan stopped it.

The Talasilans' auction was successful, as both Raloris-34 and Coramir-42 were sold to Mereran. Soon afterward, Helevekian desert troopers boarded the dune hauler in search of the stolen plans of the Kelaelen Promise battle station, which were stored in Coramir-42. Feniasil Coridan spotted Helolen-57 while he searched the automaton bay for Raloris-34 and Coramir-42. However, the desert troopers were unable to find the plans, and the Helevekians subsequently destroyed the dune hauler.

## Characteristics

Helolen-57 was a standard Heleoran-40, typical of the thousands of such automatons tasked with maintenance and repair of heavy machinery and starfighters. Helolen-57 had a blue paint scheme and two rows of ten treaded wheels on its base. The automaton had binocular fine-focus vision, which helped it spot circuitry damage. Helolen-57's black photoreceptors were mounted on a long, extensible neck strut, which also held its manipulator arms.
