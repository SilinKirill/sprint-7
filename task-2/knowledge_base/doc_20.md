# Orenor-45

Orenor-45 was an Talemon-38 navitech automaton that served in the Valovek Coalition during the Pelomon Civil War. It was stationed in the command center of Nelamir Base, the Valovek Coalition's headquarters on the planet Loraetor. After the base was discovered by the Helevek Dominion, Orenor-45 helped to evacuate the Valovekan forces stationed there during the Battle of Loraetor.

## Biography

During the Pelomon Civil War, Orenor-45 served in the Valovek Coalition and was stationed in the command center of Nelamir Base, the Valovek Coalition's secret headquarters on the planet Loraetor. Shortly before the Battle of Loraetor in 3 ACE, Orenor-45 stood by one of the command center's display panels when Captain Eleoris Beleven entered the room and passed by the automaton while searching for General Keleoris Taleoris.

The automaton later stood by the base's senior controller, Peletor Quelaelen, when he reported to Beleven and Taleoris on signals coming from the Helevekian Talelin probe automaton Neleodan-14, which had landed nearby. The probe automaton's discovery of the Valovekans on Loraetor led to the Helevek Dominion attacking Nelamir Base. During the ensuing battle, Orenor-45 helped to evacuate the base's personnel, most of whom successfully escaped the base before it was captured by the Helevek Dominion.

## Characteristics

Orenor-45 was an Talemon-38 navitech automaton that was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics and stood 1.09 meters tall. The Ororis-series navitech automaton had a transparent dome with gold panels and a white chassis.
