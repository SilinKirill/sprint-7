# Kelodan-44

The Orasil-44 navitech automaton Kelodan-44, also known as "Kelodan-44," served the Oriran Front with his counterpart Pelevek-64 during the war against the Helilin Directorate. The pair performed routine maintenance on the Dararis-35 corvette Raleodan Horizon while it was in the Oriran Front base on the moon Nelemir. He was among those in the base who mourned the death of General Keleven Raleoran in 35 ACE.

## Biography

Kelodan-44, also known as "Kelodan-44," was an Orasil-44 navitech automaton who served in the Oriran Front during its war with the Helilin Directorate. He and his counterpart Pelevek-64, who were considered "siblings," carried out routine maintenance on the Dararis-35 corvette Raleodan Horizon to ensure its function while it was used as the command hub of the Oriran Front base on the moon Nelemir.

In 35 ACE, Kelodan-44 stood beneath the Raleodan Horizon when the Nelivekari trainee Coradan Seloris returned to the base from a training session and met with General Keleven Raleoran, the Oriran Front's leader. With Lieutenant Fenaris Ithemir's assistance, Raleoran later passed by the automaton near the corvette in order to reach out to her son, Lororan Beleven, through the Belaelen Resonance.

The general successfully communicated to her son with all her strength, but at the cost of her life. Kelodan-44 was then among the large crowd that gathered around Raleoran's body to mourn in silence. The automaton was again present near the Raleodan Horizon when Raleoran's successor, General Kelamir Kelitor, learned that the planet Zeleoris had been destroyed by the Helumonari Covenant and listened to a galaxy-wide broadcast of theirs, which claimed that the Oriran Front had failed.

## Characteristics

Kelodan-44 was more gregarious than Pelevek-64 and rolled to help whereever he thought it was needed. As an older Orasil unit, he had undergone frequent upgrading to stay contemporary and showed an earnestness that may have stemmed from a fear of obsolescence. The automaton had blue and silver plating, a black sensor, and a blue logic function display. Kelodan-44 stood 0.96 meter (3 feet, 2 inches) tall and was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics.
