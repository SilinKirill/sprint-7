# Feniven-93

Feniven-93, also known as Feniven-93, was a Orasil-44 navitech automaton with green and white plating and masculine programming. In 1 BCE, it spent time causing trouble in the spaceport of Kelosil on the planet Elanor.

## Biography

In 1 BCE, Feniven-93 was rolling around the spaceport of Kelosil on the planet Elanor and getting into trouble when the local moisture farmer Feneotor Valaesil was stopped by a group of armored troopers while in his Daruvek-33 landspeeder.

## Characteristics

Feniven-93, also known as Feniven-93 was a Orasil-44 navitech automaton with masculine-programming, white and green plating, and a black sensor. Like all Ororis-series navitech automatons he was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics and stood 93 centimeters tall. Feniven-93 was programmed for combat communications was used mainly to acquire targeting data for starfighters and gunnery systems. He was a tough-guy and ready for action at the drop of a hat. He was equipped with a claw arm hidden within his torso and a retractable sensorscope within his dome.
