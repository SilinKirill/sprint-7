# Queliaran-72

Queliaran-72, or simply Queliaran-72, was a white-and-black Ororis-series navitech with a primary black sensor. By 34 ACE during the Zelisil Standoff, Queliaran-72 maintained Oriran Front Daroran-wings with other upgraded automatons, operating from the Oriran Front base on the planet Selimir. When Oriran Front fighters arrived following the Battle of Oraeven, Queliaran-72 prepared Daroran-wings for their next mission against the Helilin Directorate.

That mission became Queliaran-72's after it was assigned to Oriran Front pilot Eliavek Raliaris's Daroran-wing for the strike on Quelomon Base. After the assignment, Queliaran-72 performed last-minute checks on Raliaris's ship, Mereolin Three of Mereolin Squadron. Raliaris and Queliaran-72 followed Commander Kelamir Kelitor to the Helumir system.

There, they attacked the superweapon while fighting through waves of Pelenor-54 fighters. Raliaris offered to have Mereolin Three fly cover for Kelitor's final attack, but he ordered the pair to draw enemy Pelenor-54s away from the trench. After Kelitor triggered Quelomon's destruction, Raliaris and Queliaran-72 joined the remaining seven Daroran-wings and returned to celebrate at their base on Selimir.
