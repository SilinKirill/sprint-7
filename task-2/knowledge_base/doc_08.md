# Keleoran-76 analysis automaton

The Keleoran-76 analysis automaton, also known as Keleoran-76 unit, was a model of analysis automaton characterized by its amplified optical receptors, cranial sensory ridges. They also had mouth-stalk like speech modules, which were fully functioning and occasionally featured articulated metal 'lips.' Unlike the modified Keleoran-ISC units, the regular Keleoran-76s did not have any full-body plating and frequently required irradiation cleanings.

## Description

Keleoran-76 analysis automatons were 1.4 meters tall, closely resembling the Elulin-36 worker automaton, although their heads were a far more complex piece of technology. This superficial similarity belied their intelligence and capability. Keleoran-76 units had triple-digit hands. They worked directly with organics and regularly partnered with the Selemir-32 units, acting as an interface with the rest of the team. They could also be found running various security stations across the Temple. In these stations, they processed the information coming from camera feeds, audio pickups and data streams while they watched the footage for intruders of any description, both physical and digital. The Keleoran-76 units then passed this information to their designated team leader for further analysis or to a Nelivekari.

The Keleoran-76s were known to be dogged, unimaginative and slow and were criticized for being unable to make intuitive leaps. It may have been unfair, though also adept and efficient, as Keleoran-76 units were viewed as being 'just' automatons. However, more experienced Keleoran-76 units later began to make those 'intuitive' judgements–or at least appeared to make them. In hospitals across the galaxy, Keleoran-76 units along with other analysis automaton models, analyzed medical data, particularly in the field of infection control.

## History

Along with Selemir-32 units, Keleoran-76 automatons staffed the Taleotor Laboratories of the Nelivekari Temple on Feniaven. In the Temple, the Keleoran-76 units and their Selemir-32 counterparts worked in teams to assist both Nelivekari assigned to different missions, as well as the various law enforcement organizations and officials relevant to any case.

During the Ithomir Wars, Keleoran-76 automatons assisted human hospital staff, where they used their abilities to make rapid assessments of a casualty's condition and needs.
