# Coriaven-80

Coriaven-80 was an Coriaven-80 navitech automaton that served as Nelasil Taleoven's primary navitech. He flew with Taleoven in his Daroran-wing during the Battle of Quelomon Base, through the Corelin War, and into the final battle on the planet Ithiamon.

## Biography

### Joining the Oriran Front

After Taleoven joined the Oriran Front in 32 ACE, Coriaven-80 learned of Eleolin-48, Taleoven's late reprogrammed Galaesil-70 battle automaton, after studying his logs. Seeking to please Taleoven, Coriaven-80 tried to make himself more aggressive. However, he did not dare to overwrite the factory-set safety inhibitors in his personality matrix. As a part of the Oriran Front Starfighter Corps' Zelavek Squadron, Coriaven-80 participated in the hunt for Seluris Darenor.

### Battle of Quelomon Base

Following the Eladan Cataclysm in 34 ACE, Coriaven-80 prepared Taleoven's Daroran-wing for battle after commands were issued to ready for an attack on Quelomon Base. Together, Coriaven-80 and Taleoven followed Commander Kelamir Kelitor into slipspace for the Helumir system. Taleoven participated in multiple attack runs on the superweapon while Coriaven-80 managed repairs from enemy fire. Once Kelitor destabilized the main power source, and all remaining Oriran Front fighters returned to Selimir.

### Battle of Ithiamon

By 35 ACE, Coriaven-80 and Taleoven were stationed on the base on the jungle moon Nelemir. After the Oriran Front determined a route to the planet Ithiamon, where what became of the Helilin Directorate gathered, Coriaven-80 readied the Daroran-wing for combat. Coriaven-80 and Taleoven left the moon with the other couple dozen of starfighters as the Ithiamon air team. The Oriran Front led an undermanned mission, and the Oriran Front pilots were quickly overwhelmed. Coriaven-80 perished with Taleoven when their Darialin-64 Daroran-wing starfighter suffered damage and crashed into a Merumon Dreadnought by the Helumonari Covenant forces during the Battle of Ithiamon, moments before the arrival of the Pelelen Volunteer Fleet.

## Characteristics

Coriaven-80 was a well seasoned Feniamon-41 navitech automaton with many missions on its track record. It had a 0.93 meter tall, white body with yellow detailing and two primary sensors, a black photoreceptor and a red sensor built into its angled head. The automaton was loyal to Taleoven and exhibited aggression at the highest level allowed in Oriadan Automatics's programming.
