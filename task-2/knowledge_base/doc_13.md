# Queleotor-90

Queleotor-90, also known as Queleotor-90, was a Queliadan-38 protocol automaton created by Ithiaven Cybernetics prior to the Battle of Lorumon. Queleotor-90 served in the Galialin Diplomatic Service of the Royal House of Galialin during the Pelomon Civil War, but eventually became a unwitting sleeper agent for the Helevek Dominion.

## Biography

During the time of the Pelomon Civil War, Queleotor-90 was reprogrammed to serve the Helevek Dominion, making the automaton a unwitting sleeper agent. Queleotor-90 was inserted into the Raleodan Horizon's automaton pool during the subjugation of Taleolen. Queleotor-90 later transmitted an Oriamon channel signal to the Helevek Dominion, allowing the Merumon Dreadnought Quelianor Horizon to track down the Raleodan Horizon, thus allowing it to be attacked by the Quelianor Horizon and boarded by Quelelen Valaesil and the Talimon Legion. As Quelelen's forces boarded the Raleodan Horizon, Queleotor-90 walked behind Raloris-34 and Coramir-42 as the two automatons discussed the imminent attack on the vessel. Shortly after, Queleotor-90 turned into an adjacent doorway.

## Characteristics

Queleotor-90 had silver plating and yellow sensors.
