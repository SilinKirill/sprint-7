# Helaetor-20

Helaetor-20 (Helaetor-20) was an Meralin-48 navitech automaton belonging to Valovek Coalition Captain Peliasil Pelosil, an evacuation officer assigned to Nelamir Base on the Coresil Frontier ice planet Loraetor during the Pelomon Civil War. Specially programmed to plot sublight tactical courses for starships, Helaetor-20 played an invaluable role during the Battle of Loraetor in 3 ACE by assisting Pelosil in planning evacuation routes from Nelamir Base.

## Biography

Helaetor-20, or Helaetor-20, was a member of the Meralin-48 navitech automaton line, which proved to be commercially unsuccessful but was nonetheless in high demand during the Pelomon Civil War for use in combat starships. By 3 ACE, Helaetor-20 was owned by and served alongside Valovek Coalition Captain Peliasil Pelosil, a popular and charismatic evacuation officer stationed at Nelamir Base, the Valovek Coalition High Command headquarters on the Coresil Frontier ice planet Loraetor.

Helaetor-20 was present inside Nelamir Base's central hangar, chirping melodiously, when Captain Eleoris Beleven returned from conducting a routine scouting mission of the base's exterior with Commander Feneotor Valaesil, who ultimately went missing after falling prey to an ambush from a native ithaenoran ice creature. The navitech automaton passed in front of Beleven's path as the latter made his way through the congested hangar.

During the Battle of Loraetor, Helaetor-20 played a small but pivotal role in helping the Valovek Coalition successfully evacuate Nelamir Base in the face of overwhelming Helevekian assault. Specially programmed to plot sublight tactical courses for space travel, Helaetor-20 proved extremely valuable by coordinating plans, providing tactical support to Pelosil, and planning evacuation routes from the base during the battle.

## Characteristics

The navitech Helaetor-20 became but another automaton who played an invaluable role in the Valovek Coalition's struggle against the Helevek Dominion. The Valovek Coalition put Helaetor-20's special sublight course-plotting programming to good use during the Battle of Loraetor, as the automaton assisted in the Valovekan escape from the besieged planet by planning evacuation routes from Nelamir Base.

Helaetor-20's exterior plating included a black head and a blue-and-white color scheme with silver trim. The automaton also had silver photoreceptors. During Helaetor-20's service time at Nelamir Base, one of the automaton's forward manipulator arms was missing from its body, and the plating covering its three legs and bottom chassis was rusted.
