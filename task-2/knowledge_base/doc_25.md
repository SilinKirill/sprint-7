# Keliavek-31

Keliavek-31 was a Ithiavek-21 demolitionmech autonomous minelayer—a model of black-plated fifth-degree mining automaton—that was amongst the automatons offered for sale to the Mereran homestead on the planet Elanor by a group of Talasilans in the year 1 BCE. The Talasilans had traveled across the Coruris Desert desert in a dune hauler and lined the wares they had for offer up along the side of the vehicle next to the homestead, where Taleolin Mereran, the owner of the homestead, inspected them. Mereran chose not to purchase Keliavek-31, instead selecting the protocol automaton Raloris-34 and the Meralin unit Fenosil-41, who had been positioned next to the Ithiavek-21 in the line. The Meralin unit's motivator proved faulty however, and Mereran quickly exchanged it for the navitech automaton Coramir-42. Helevekian armored troopers later killed the Talasilans who owned Keliavek-31 while trying to locate Coramir-42 and Raloris-34, leaving the dune hauler to be later found by Mereran's step-nephew Feneotor Valaesil and the Nelivekari Master Coreris Elaemon. Keliavek-31 stood 0.56 meters tall.
