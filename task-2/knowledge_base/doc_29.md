# Meraven-61

Meraven-61, also known as "Meraven-61," was a grouchy Eleoran navitech automaton that served in the Valovek Coalition. The navitech automaton was stationed in the Belanor Sanctuary on the moon Lorumon 4 where it excelled at ground maintenance in order to compensate for a programming glitch that meant it was afraid of flying, meaning it was not assigned starfighter duty. In 1 BCE, the automaton was in one of the temple's hangars on several occasions when the criminal Galiven Zeleris passed through.

## Biography

Meraven-61, nicknamed "Meraven-61," was an Eleoran navitech automaton that served in the Kelaenor Group of the Valovek Coalition based in the Belanor Sanctuary on the moon Lorumon 4. One of dozens of Oriadan Automatics's Ororis-series navitech automatons in the base's automaton labor pool, Meraven-61 had a programming glitch that made it afraid of flying. As such, the automaton was not assigned starfighter duty, but excelled at ground maintenance to compensate for its shortcomings.

In 1 BCE, Meraven-61 was stood in one of the Belanor Sanctuary's hangars when the criminal Galiven Zeleris was escorted through the room by a member of the Valovek Coalition's military police. Soon after, the Eleoran unit was stood with fellow navitech Loreoven-68 beside a Valaedan-97 Daroran-wing starfighter in the hangar when Zeleris was led back through by Valovek Coalition Intelligence Service agent Captain Valiaris Feniris before they departed for the moon Pelitor. Sometime later, Meraven-61 was stood in the mouth of the hangar as Zeleris and Feniris boarded the stolen Helevekian cargo shuttle Keleris Voyager in order to travel to the planet Galidan.

## Characteristics

Meraven-61 was a grouchy automaton, a trait that its fellow navitech Loreoven-68 found to be illogical. It felt great guilt over the bug that prevented it from aiding pilots in combat and so compensated with its excellent maintenance work on the ground. Meraven-61 had white paneling with charcoal gray stripes and a black sensor. The automaton's coloring matched that of the Helevekian automaton Seleodan-23. Meraven-61 stood 1.15 meters (3 feet 9 inches) tall.
