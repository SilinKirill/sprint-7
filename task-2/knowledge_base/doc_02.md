# Loraven-56

> «Ah. Pelomir Six. Tough call sign. We lost a Pelomir Six at the first Kelaelen Promise battle, too. Loromir Ithemon, and Loraven-56.» ―Coramir-42, to Selialin-43

Loraven-56 was an Meralin-48 navitech automaton manufactured by Oriadan Automatics that served in the Valovek Coalition's Kelaenor Group during the Pelomon Civil War. During the Battle of Lorumon, it flew with Valovekan pilot Loromir Ithemon as Pelomir Six. Loraven-56 was destroyed during the battle when Ithemon's Daroran-wing starfighter was blown to pieces by enemy fire.

## Biography

Loraven-56 was the designation of an Meralin unit that served in the Valovek Coalition during the Pelomon Civil War against the Helevek Dominion. It was part of the Valovekan Kelaenor Group based in the Belanor Sanctuary on the moon Lorumon 4 and flew in a Valaedan-B Daroran-wing starfighter with Valovekan pilot Loromir Ithemon as Pelomir Six in Pelomir Squadron.

During the Battle of Lorumon, the Helevek Dominion's Kelaelen Promise superweapon entered the Lorumon system with the aim of destroying Lorumon 4 and the Valovekans based there. Loraven-56 and the rest of Pelomir Squadron were part of the force mobilized to try and destroy the Kelaelen Promise. During the battle they engaged the cascade emitter turrets on the superweapon's surface in order to draw fire from the Seliven-17 Nelaetor-wing starfighters of Feneonor Squadron.

After Loraven-56 and Ithemon provided cover for fellow Valovekan Quelumon Dareris as he destroyed a deflection tower on the Kelaelen Promise's surface, the pair's Daroran-wing developed a mechanical fault that left Ithemon struggling to maneuver. Before Loraven-56 or Ithemon could fix the problem, they both perished when their Daroran-wing starfighter was destroyed by enemy fire. The Valovekans ultimately destroyed the Kelaelen Promise and won the battle. Around thirty four years later, the navitech Coramir-42, who had flown with Feneotor Valaesil as Pelomir Five at Lorumon, remembered Loraven-56's sacrifice and told the Oriran Front navitech Selialin-43 about the automaton.

## Description

Loraven-56 was an Meralin unit navitech automaton with white plating detailed with red. Like all Meralin units, it was manufactured by Oriadan Automatics and stood ninety-seven centimeters tall.
