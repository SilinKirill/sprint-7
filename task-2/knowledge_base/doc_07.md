# Peliris-13

Peliris-13 was a Corimon-18 secretary/business communications automaton that was paired with Helemon-71, another automaton of the same model. After a crash left him abandoned on the planet Elanor, Peliris-13 became separated from Helemon-71 and ended up in a pile of scrap on board a Talasilan dune hauler, where he encountered the navitech automaton Coramir-42 in 1 BCE.

## Biography

Peliris-13 was the designation of a Corimon-18 secretary/business communications automaton manufactured by Talomir Robotics, Inc.. He was twinned with fellow Corimon unit Helemon-71 in service to members of the Galunoran species.

After a crash in the deserts of the planet Elanor, Peliris-13 was abandoned and became separated from Helemon-71. He then broke down and was captured by Talasilan scavengers. In 1 BCE, the secretary automaton was on board the dune hauler of the Talasilan Chief Selaven Taliator's tribe when the navitech automaton Coramir-42 was brought on board after being captured in the Nelisil Wastes.

As the navitech rolled past Peliris-13 while surveying his surroundings, the secretary automaton swayed back and forth amidst a heap of scrap. Coramir-42 was later sold when the dune hauler stopped at the Mereran moisture farm, after which Helevekian armored troopers massacred Selaven Taliator's tribe and left its dune hauler abandoned in the desert.

## Characteristics

Peliris-13 was modified from a standard Corimon-18 automaton to more closely resemble the Galunoran he once served, with black photoreceptors made to look like Galunoran eyes. The automaton's hands were human-style manipulators with five digits. By 1 BCE, Peliris-13 was ancient with locomotors too broken and sand-encrusted for him to stand or move properly. Despite this, he remained optimistic and hoped to soon be repaired and sold. Peliris-13 had white plating that was stained black or brown in many areas.
